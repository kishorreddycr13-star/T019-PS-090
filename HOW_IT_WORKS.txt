================================================================================
HOW THE OTT RIGHTS & ROYALTY ENGINE WORKS (A SIMPLE GUIDE)
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Think of this project like the financial and legal brain of an OTT streaming 
platform (like Netflix, Disney+ Hotstar, or Prime Video).

When an OTT platform streams movies and TV shows, it does NOT own all of them. 
Instead, it licenses them from studios/production houses (Licensors) under strict 
contracts. 

This engine automatically does three big jobs:
  1. Checks if a movie is legally allowed to play in a country right now.
  2. Tracks who watched what, for how long, and filters out spam or short clicks.
  3. Calculates the exact money owed to each studio every month (subscription 
     revenue, ad revenue, taxes, advances, and splits down to the last paisa).

================================================================================
3. DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF EACH STEP
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: LICENCE & WINDOW VALIDATION (Are the contracts valid?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before any title can be shown on the platform, its contract must be verified:
- Date Validity: Window start date must be before or equal to window end date.
- Exclusivity: If Studio A has an EXCLUSIVE licence for Title X in India from 
  June to August, Studio B cannot have a licence for that same title in India 
  during overlapping dates.
- Statutory Holdbacks: 
  * A movie cannot stream on OTT until 45 days after its Cinema release.
  * A TV show cannot stream on OTT until 7 days after its TV premiere.
* Result: Creates `licence_validation.csv` (shows ACCEPTED or REJECTED).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: PLAYBACK ELIGIBILITY (Can the user watch right now?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a user clicks "Play", the system checks:
- Timezones: OTT contracts specify dates in the territory's local time (IST in 
  India, EDT/EST in the US, SGT in Singapore, GST in UAE). The engine converts 
  UTC server timestamps into local territory time.
- Midnight Straddles: A title is playable right up to 23:59:59 on its end date. 
  If a user starts watching at 23:59:59, the system gives a 4-hour grace buffer 
  so they can finish the movie even after midnight.
* Result: Playback is marked ALLOWED or DENIED.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: VIEW TRACKING & METERING (Which views actually count?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Not every click on a video is a valid view:
- Qualifying Threshold: A view only counts for revenue if the user watched for 
  at least 120 seconds (2 minutes), OR at least half the total runtime for very 
  short videos.
- 24-Hour Deduplication: If the same subscriber re-watches the same movie 
  multiple times within 24 hours, it is counted as ONE view session.
- Late Views: Views must be ingested before the monthly cutoff (the 10th of the 
  following month). Late views are tagged as LATE_VIEW and moved into next 
  month's settlement.
* Result: Creates `view_decisions.csv` (shows whether each view was counted).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: REVENUE POOL CALCULATION (How much money is in the pot?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Money comes from two streams:
1. Subscription Pool (SVOD - Premium):
   - 60% of total territory subscription fees are put into a royalty pool.
   - Each title gets a share of this pool based on its proportion of total 
     qualifying view-hours watched that month.
2. Advertisement Pool (AVOD - Free with Ads):
   - 55% of territory ad revenue is put into an ad royalty pool.
   - Each title gets a share based on its proportion of ad impressions served.
* Result: Creates `title_shares.csv` (monthly pool and ad shares per title).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: ROYALTY SETTLEMENT & TAXATION (How much does each studio get paid?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once a title's gross share is known, the engine calculates the final payment:
- Minimum Guarantee (MG): Some studios are promised an upfront advance payment 
  (e.g., Rs 100,000) in Month 1. In subsequent months, their gross earnings are 
  used to "recoup" (pay back) this advance before they receive extra cash.
- Payment Caps: Some contracts have a maximum lifetime payout cap. Once reached, 
  status becomes CAPPED, and excess earnings are retained by the platform.
- Withholding Tax (TDS):
  * Domestic Indian Licensors: 10% statutory tax is deducted.
  * Foreign Licensors: 20% statutory tax, or discounted treaty rates (e.g., 15% 
    under DTAA tax treaties) where registered.
- Co-Licensor Splits with Zero Leakage: If a movie is co-owned by 3 partners 
  (e.g. 33.33%, 33.33%, 33.34%), money is split to exact paise (2 decimal places). 
  Any 1-paisa rounding fraction is given to the largest split partner so no 
  money is lost.
* Result: Creates `settlements.csv` (exact net payable amount per licensor).


================================================================================
4. THE 4 FINAL OUTPUT FILES (DELIVERABLES)
================================================================================
When `python run_engine.py` finishes, it writes 4 CSV files into `evaluation_outputs/`:

1. `licence_validation.csv`:
   Lists every contract request and whether it was ACCEPTED or REJECTED.

2. `view_decisions.csv`:
   Lists every single play event, local watch time, whether it qualified, and 
   whether it counted.

3. `title_shares.csv`:
   Monthly breakdown by territory of subscription view-hours, pool shares, ad 
   impressions, and total title revenue.

4. `settlements.csv`:
   The monthly financial ledger for each studio: Gross share, MG advances, 
   recoupment, tax deducted (TDS), and final Net Payable amount.


================================================================================
5. KEY STRENGTHS OF THIS ENGINE
================================================================================
- Mathematical Accuracy: Uses Python `Decimal` (never floating-point numbers) 
  so calculations never suffer from rounding errors.
- Timezone Smart: Correctly handles daylight savings and local offsets (IST, 
  EDT, SGT, GST).
- Full Audit Trail: Every transaction and settlement step is cryptographically 
  hashed (SHA-256) into a relational database (`ott_engine.db`) for compliance.
- Fast & Automated: Processes thousands of view events and dozens of contracts 
  in seconds via `run_engine.py`.
